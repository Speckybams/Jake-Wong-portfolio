Waiting For Time

Don't wait for time. Make it wait for you.

Waiting For Time is a proof of concept film where the audience embarks on a high stake mission derailed by a universal collision with a parallel one. Feel the ambience and tension-building atmosphere while you experience the extraordinary events of two youths unfold in the heart of Paris, France.

Green light is emitted by tactical chemlights used by SWAT and special forces around the world. It is the most easily detectable wavelength of light to the human eye. During an interuniversal collision, electromagnetic waves are scattered through space in an unpredictable manner. Common laws of physics are altered in severities depending on the nature of the colliding universe. In this case, an antiverse has all of our known physics constants but inversed.

Director: Jake Wong

Producer: Gregory Nagaev

Cast: Leo Barros Jones, Lou Muller, Savva Nagaev

Production Company: Causality Films

Year: 2022
